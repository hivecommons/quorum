# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues,
pull requests, or discussions.

Report it privately by email to **security@hivecommons.dev**, the contact
published in the organization's
[security.txt](https://hivecommons.dev/.well-known/security.txt). If that
address is unreachable, contact a repository maintainer directly (see the
maintainers listed on the [hivecommons organization](https://github.com/hivecommons)).

Please include:

- The affected component and the commit or release version.
- A description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce, with any proof-of-concept or logs.

Do not include live meeting audio, transcripts or tokens; redact them.

## What to Expect

We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 business days, investigate, and
coordinate a fix and disclosure timeline with you. Please allow a reasonable
time to remediate before public disclosure.
